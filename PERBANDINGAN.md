# ?? PERBANDINGAN DESIGN LAMA vs BARU

## ? RINGKASAN PERUBAHAN:

### **DESIGN LAMA** (V1 - Futuristik)
- ? Background dark gradient (#1B132B ? #281C3C)
- ? Glassmorphism cards (blur effect)
- ? Neon purple glow effects
- ? Terlalu ramai dengan efek visual
- ? Dark theme yang terlalu "keren"

### **DESIGN BARU** (V2 - Clean Professional) ?
- ? Background putih/terang (#FFFFFF)
- ? Cards minimalis dengan border simple
- ? Typography professional (Space Grotesk)
- ? Fokus pada data & readability
- ? Clean seperti Stockwise.id
- ? Warna ungu lebih subtle
- ? Layout spacious & breathable

---

## ?? PERUBAHAN DETAIL:

### **1. COLORS:**
**LAMA:**
- Dark background: #1B132B
- Glassmorphism: rgba blur
- Neon glow: 0 0 40px purple

**BARU:**
- Light background: #FFFFFF
- Card background: #FFFFFF
- Border: #E5E7EB (soft gray)
- Shadows: Subtle, tidak berlebihan

---

### **2. TYPOGRAPHY:**
**LAMA:**
- Inter only
- Weights tidak konsisten

**BARU:**
- **Headings:** Space Grotesk (700)
- **Body:** Inter (400-600)
- Line height lebih baik (1.6-1.8)

---

### **3. CARDS:**
**LAMA:**
- Glassmorphism blur
- Neon glow on hover
- Dark transparent

**BARU:**
- White solid background
- Simple border (1px)
- Subtle shadow
- Clean hover effect (lift + border color)

---

### **4. BUTTONS:**
**LAMA:**
- Gradient background
- Glow effect
- Glass effect

**BARU:**
- Solid purple (#6800FC)
- Outline variant
- Simple hover state
- No glow

---

### **5. NAVIGATION:**
**LAMA:**
- Dark blur background
- Glassmorphism

**BARU:**
- White with subtle blur
- Clean border bottom
- Professional look

---

### **6. LAYOUT:**
**LAMA:**
- Tight spacing
- Banyak efek visual

**BARU:**
- Generous whitespace
- Focus on content
- Spacious & breathable
- Grid system lebih baik

---
